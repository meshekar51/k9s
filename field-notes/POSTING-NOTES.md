# Posting notes

## What is here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `README.md` | The long-form article. Renders at the `field-notes/` URL of this repo. |
| `linkedin-post.txt` | The feed post, plain text, paste with no editing. 2,278 characters. |

## Which LinkedIn format

`linkedin-post.txt` targets a **feed post**, not the long-form article editor.

- Limit is 3,000 characters. This draft is 2,278, leaving room to add your own
  opening line.
- It contains no markdown, because LinkedIn does not render any. The `→`
  characters are literal and will display correctly.
- The fold is at roughly 210 characters. The popeye claim lands above it, which
  is the whole reason it opens there.

## Before you post

- The article is written in a neutral, verified-reference voice on purpose — it
  claims no war stories, because none were verified. **If you have a real
  incident where one of these keys mattered, add it.** A concrete "this cost me
  an afternoon" is the strongest thing you can put in the post, and it has to be
  yours, not invented.
- The closing question is genuine and answerable. Keep a question, but swap it
  for your own if this one does not sound like you.
- Hashtags are five, at the very end.
